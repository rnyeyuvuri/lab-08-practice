RIP Doomsday
doomsday was very sad, at the end of the day he went to bed and dreampt of bubbling toil which spread throughout the ocean, which had,by now, become lost to ink, reminders of octopi, which had fended for themselves, centuries ago.
From when her life started in woe, through the thickets she shall sow. 
The glimmer that brightened her silver hair would reach its head only once more, how long before he no longer soars.
She sits and thinks of black skies, and how one day they'll emerge with heat, large orange waves, which dot the blotted night sky. There she sees, upon the wake, a cruch-feld santum, on the range, o' sing thee of king of morrows bend, how sorrow, we follow, till the end. Livid time, we rememthee, the time we lived we used to be, and once tommorow's sun again my rise, again again, we do due time.
